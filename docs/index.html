It composes, it does not send

There is no mail server here. Messages are prepared on your device and opened in your own mail client — so nothing is sent that you have not seen.

Every figure has a source

A placeholder that cannot be traced to a column is refused rather than left blank, and a row that fails is excluded with its reason named.
